The explorer's field guide

Every feature
is a real number.

ContinentsSix sectors, five large US companies each. Each continent has its own colour.
MountainsOne per company. Height is price growth since 2007 on a log scale. Colour is this week's return red down, green up; bigger moves glow.
Continental driftAverage correlation between sectors over 30 trading days. Calm markets: sectors move independently and continents spread out. Crises: everything falls together and they collide into a supercontinent — diversification fails when it is needed most.
WeatherMarket fear (VIX). Higher fear brings storm fronts, a red atmosphere, a dimmer sun, and lightning above 40.
Volcanoes & quakesWeeks when the average stock fell more than 7%. The planet shakes and volcanoes erupt on the two sectors that fell hardest.
AI explorer's logExplanations generated by AI from only the numbers shown, labelled as such. Without a connection, a summary is built from the data.
LimitsPrice growth, not market cap. Today's big companies, so survivors only. Not a trading tool, not financial advice.
