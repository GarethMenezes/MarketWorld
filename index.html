    Every feature is a real number.

    ContinentsSix sectors of selected companies in the active UK, US or Asian market. These baskets are not official market indexes. Name shown on the continent.
    MountainsOne per company, ticker above the peak. Height = price growth since 2007 (log scale). Glow at the foot = this week's return red down, green up.
    Sea levelAverage correlation between sectors. Calm: normal sea, six separate continents. As sectors start moving together the sea falls and wide salt flats appear around every coast; at correlation 0.85+ the sea is at its lowest in this visual model. Pale blue marks shallow water and tan shelves mark exposed seabed. The optional white outline marks the normal coastline. The scale illustrates correlation, not real sea height.
    WeatherUS VIX drives storms and sunlight. It is a US-market measure used as a global-risk proxy for the UK and Asia, not a local volatility index.
